# Dark Veil reference fidelity

Use Dark Veil itself as the active visual target. Persona, Cowboy Bebop and Last Call styling belong to the superseded v0.1.0 direction.

The observed reference uses a moonlit, fog-layered ruined forest, dense angular tree crowns, textured stone and irregular ground detail. Warm braziers and cold moonlight organize the image. Preserve the original scene's silhouettes, camera scale and material atlas before inventing substitutes.

The title uses bone-colored stacked Rye lettering, the horned skull, gold ornament and framed menu actions. The First Hand uses the recovered red velvet table/dealer composition, aged parchment and illustrated weapon cards. The HUD keeps wave information above, circular vitality and resource meters below left, and weapon/ammunition below right. Menus should remain responsive, keyboard accessible and interruptible. Respect reduced motion.

Locally recovered reference assets and captured world geometry are documented in [ASSET-RECOVERY.md](reference/ASSET-RECOVERY.md). They are not newly authored project artwork and are not implicitly MIT/CC BY. Keep their provenance and ignored storage boundaries intact. Do not substitute a concept illustration for a rendered gameplay comparison.

A recovered mesh is not a finished scene: verify normals, atlas coordinates, color space, lighting, camera, shadows and atmosphere in the actual engine. Captured actors are static data until reconstructed animation is implemented; inferred pivots must remain labeled as inferred. Use screenshots to record the remaining mismatch rather than claiming 1:1 parity from asset extraction alone.
